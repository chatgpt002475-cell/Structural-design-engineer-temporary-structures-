### 3. Design Criteria & Load Parameters
1. **Vertical Loads:**
   - Dead load of permanent structural elements (kN/m or kN/m2)
   - Self-weight of formwork and falsework components
   - Construction live load (min 1.5 to 2.5 kN/m2 as per IRC:87 / IS 14687)
   - Dynamic impact factors (e.g., concrete pumping surge, segment lowering)
2. **Lateral Loads:**
   - Minimum mandatory horizontal lateral load = max(2.5% vertical load, wind + 1%)
   - Wind load based on IS 875 (Part 3) with temporary risk coefficient k1
   - Water current / wave forces for marine temporary works
   - Breaking, traction, and slewing loads from erection plant
3. **Geotechnical / Foundation Criteria:**
   - Verified Safe Bearing Capacity (SBC) from Plate Load Test (kPa)
   - Maximum allowable settlement (total < 10mm, differential < 5mm)
   - Ground water table elevation and dewatering requirements
